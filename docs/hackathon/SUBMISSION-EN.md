## Tagline
Your Solana wallet, alive.

## Short description
Walletkin turns confirmed Solana wallet activity into a living, explainable creature on Seeker. Stable Genesis DNA gives each wallet an origin; observed activity adds memories, organic traits and personal lore.

## Product description
Wallet history is useful but rarely personal. Walletkin turns it into an identity users can recognize, revisit and share.

Connect through Mobile Wallet Adapter, reveal deterministic Genesis DNA, and meet a creature drawn from thirteen distinct lineages with six inherited color forms. Confirmed successful activity adds timestamped memories and evidence-based traits. Story explains the biography; Data exposes the rules and scan limits.

My Colony keeps saved wallet identities together on the device. Native sharing exports a creature card. Rescan checks newly observed history and updates the biography. The Legends archive showcases the same thirteen ordinary lineages plus one Anomaly with five catalogue forms.

## Solana Mobile integration
Native Kotlin Android, real Mobile Wallet Adapter connect/reconnect and mainnet RPC analysis. Verified Seeker Genesis Token ownership/extension checks create a locally permanent Seekerborn mark. The submitted APK was run on the builder's Seeker; the 75-second English-captioned demo is edited from that device recording.

## SKR integration — special prize
SKR is part of the creature's biography and monetization design. Observed SKR history can establish a persistent Resonance Mark/Core. Positive verified active stake activates free Resonance effects; verified zero stake makes dynamic effects dormant without erasing the earned mark. Unavailable checks remain unknown.

For the MVP, Premium eligibility requires at least 100 actively staked SKR and the intended 30-day plan costs 100 SKR. Premium does not change DNA or rarity. The Android checkout and Node verification backend are implemented in source: wallet-confirmed checked SKR transfer, unique memo, exact finalized payment verification and transaction reuse prevention. HTTPS backend deployment and a real mainnet payment test remain pending, so the submission does not claim live purchases.

The Store contains eight clearly labeled cosmetic previews; individual item prices are not yet configured. Implemented Premium exports are HD PNG cards and transparent avatars. Future discounted 3/6/12-month plans and advanced benefits are planned.

## Traits and visual identity
Painted organic material integrations cover all thirteen normal species, independently gated by evidence: SKR Mark/Core, Active Resonance, Seekerborn, Jupiter Navigator, JUP Orbit Keeper and NFT Collector. JUP governance staking is analyzed separately from Jupiter protocol interaction. Preview combinations are illustrative and are never saved as wallet evidence.

NFT Collector indicates observed successful NFT protocol interaction, not proven ownership of collections. Each lineage has distinct accepted artwork, basic motion and tap reactions. Richer animation is planned.

## Innovation and return use
A wallet's public activity shapes its biography while deterministic origin stays stable. Users can see why a trait appears rather than receive an unexplained rarity score. Our retention hypothesis is that people return after actual wallet activity to discover memories, update their Colony and share their identity. We do not claim measured retention, revenue or traction.
